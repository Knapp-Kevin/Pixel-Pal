# Pixel Pals Vertical Slice Art Inventory

## Status

The repository now contains a functional living-world art prototype for the first vertical slice.

This is production-structured prototype art. It is suitable for validating composition, customization, readability, interaction, and technical integration. It is not the final polished storybook art set.

## Implemented Living-World Art

### Pal body families

- Fox
- Deer
- Rabbit

Each body family has a distinct head, ears, tail treatment, and silhouette.

### Pal palettes

### Accessories

### Expressions

- Content

### Poses

- Playful

### Named resident

Fern has a fixed Rabbit appearance using the Mosscream palette, Ear Dapple markings, and Leaf Pin accessory.

### Willowmere environment

The current scene includes:

- layered hills;
- winding village path;
- two home exteriors;
- Great Tree;
- village bench;
- flower garden;
- persistent shared-play stone marker;
- persistent Great Tree friendship ribbons.

## Implemented Integration

The tablet app now visually supports:

- live first-Pal preview;
- species selection;
- palette selection;
- marking selection;
- accessory selection;
- first-Pal rendering after world creation;
- Fern rendering;
- activity-specific expressions;
- playful and standing poses;
- visible village change after the first activity;
- visible Great Tree change after the friendship ceremony.

## Not Yet Final

The current art does not yet include:

- final painterly storybook illustrations;
- professionally refined anatomy and line work;
- final canonical Fern design approval;
- child, adult, and elder body stages;
- sitting, sleeping, walking, carrying, hugging, or working poses;
- home interiors;
- animated transitions;
- seasonal variants;
- regional lineage variants;
- mixed-lineage phenotype testing;
- final clothing fit across all body families;
- export-sized raster assets;
- complete asset provenance records;
- final performance testing on the oldest target tablet.

## Next Art Gate

The prototype art is successful when children can:

1. recognize Fox, Deer, and Rabbit without labels;
2. recognize their named first Pal after customization;
3. distinguish their Pal from Fern immediately;
4. understand the emotional difference between expressions;
5. notice the play stones after the first activity;
6. notice the friendship ribbons after the Great Tree ceremony;
7. remember both Pals and the scene the following day.

## Production Recommendation

Use this vector art system as a layout, behavior, and compatibility reference.

The next art investment should refine one complete path to near-final quality:

- one approved first-Pal body family;
- Fern;
- Willowmere village scene;
- Great Tree ceremony scene;
- one home exterior and interior;
- one storybook memory illustration.

That focused quality pass will reveal whether the final living-world style can retain the warmth of the existing storybook references without sacrificing modularity and tablet performance.
